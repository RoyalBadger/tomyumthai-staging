Privacy & SMS Policy

Tom Yum Thai Restaurant • 3313 Belt Line Rd, Garland, TX 75044 • Effective August 31, 2026

SMS / Text Message Program

Program name: Tom Yum Thai Order Updates.

What we send: transactional text messages about an order you placed — an order confirmation and an alert when your order is ready for pickup or out for delivery. Typically 1–3 messages per order. We do not send marketing or promotional texts through this program.

How you opt in: during online checkout, you may check an unchecked box labeled “Text me updates about this order.” We only send texts to customers who check that box, and your consent is recorded with your order. Checking the box is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

How you opt out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help, or call us at (214) 703‑0391.

Cost: message and data rates may apply per your mobile carrier's plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Information We Collect

  • Order details — the items you order and your customizations.
  • Contact details — your name and mobile phone number; your email address and delivery address only if you provide them.
  • Payment — processed by Stripe, our payment provider. Your card number never touches our systems.

How We Use It

Only to prepare and fulfill your order, contact you about it, and send the text updates you opted into. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your phone number or opt-in data with third parties for their marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Retention

Personal contact details attached to orders are retained for up to 90 days for order-support purposes and then removed from our systems.
